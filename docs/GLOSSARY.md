## 1. How to read this glossary

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Term | The canonical term in documents and code |
| Definition | Our meaning. Where a formula matters it is given exactly. |
| UI label (D-02) | The label users see. "—" means the term does not appear in the UI. |
| Legacy eRev term | The exact legacy string (button, template column or `Contract_Live` column) or "none" |
| RightRev term (reference only) | For research traceability only. These strings never appear in eRev product UI, API names or copy (G§5, D-02, REQ-UX-010). |
| ASC / IFRS reference | Topic paragraph and the IFRS 15 (or other IFRS) counterpart. "—" where the term is a product construct. |

Codification references follow research 04's source register. Paragraph numbers are verified against the Codification by the revenue accountant per D-26.

## 2. Sign and display conventions (D-12, D-15, D-16)

| Quantity | Convention | Where used |
|---|---|---|
| Net position | `net_position = cumulative consideration billed or unconditionally due − cumulative revenue recognized`, per combination group, per contracting entity, per book. Positive: contract liability. Negative: contract asset and/or unbilled receivable. It is the balance of the CONTRACT_LIABILITY control role before the period-end reclass (POLICIES ALG-02). Financing interest accreted under JET-11 is part of it (D-76; CHK-137); refund liabilities, deposit liabilities and the other balances ALG-02 excludes are not (D-15). | Engine, answer keys (asserted as labelled balances) |
| UI display | The UI never shows a signed position. It shows labelled balances: "Contract liability 1,200.00", "Contract asset 300.00", "Unbilled receivable 90.19". | Every screen and standard report |
| Legacy 71-column export | Uses the legacy column names and the legacy sign. Legacy `Current Contract Position - POB` and `- Contract Level` = billing cumulative − revenue cumulative, the same sign as D-12, so no inversion is applied. | REQ-RPT-012, REQ-RPT-013 (D-33) |
| Research 04 §0.2 and research 06 §3.7 | Their opposite convention (revenue − consideration; positive = contract asset) and the harness sign inversion are superseded by D-12 and must not be implemented. | Background only |
| Subledger and journal lines | Debit positive, credit negative, in transaction and functional currency. Every debit and credit pair derives from one rounded amount (D-16). | Subledger, journals, JE population export |
| Posted amounts | Rounded to the currency's ISO 4217 minor unit with ROUND_HALF_UP; schedules use cumulative rounding of the exact cumulative amount (D-11a); apportionments use largest remainder (D-11). | Schedules, journals, balances |
| Negative numbers in UI | One tenant-wide style, minus sign or parentheses; the default is parentheses (REQ-UX-006; D-75). | UI and XLSX exports |
