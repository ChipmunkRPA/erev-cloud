POLICIES ALG-02 step 5 (`:486`, POL-121 `POB_DEBIT_POSITIONS`) attributes the netting unit's debit position U to obligations by classification and weights: unbilled receivable "over unconditional POBs with weights max(0, R_p − B_p)", contract asset "over conditional POBs with the same weights", with fallback chains by posted allocation and resolved SSP; POL-120 fixes the netting unit at contract, entity and book, with combined contracts as one contract (the property worlds' group).
