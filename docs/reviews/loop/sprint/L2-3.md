### Spec questions

- **L2-3-Q-1 (`apply` signature and the price function).** Table 0.2-A `apply(ctx, st, ev, tb)` gives stage 06 no SSP books and no price function.

  - Decision: ΔVC = the change of `allocation_basis.posted` between `price_at(d, before = ev)` and `price_at(d, before = next event)`, less Σ ΔC_m.
  - S06-INV-01 then checks Σ a_posted after the event against the price after it, which also tests the state before the event.

  - After merge, re-evaluating the stage 09 `MODIFICATION` cause nodes against the cited `catch_up@` nodes exposes this for time-elapsed obligations. Units measures agree: the CHK-028 test re-evaluates the joint trace.
  - Proposed: stage 09 measures time-elapsed boundary deltas at d − 1 (lane L2-4 scope, or a next-level fix batch under D-81).

  - Decision: RQ⁰_p is `totals.quantity` less removals, so `resolve_ssp(at = d, quantity = RQ⁰_p)` prices the remaining increments.
