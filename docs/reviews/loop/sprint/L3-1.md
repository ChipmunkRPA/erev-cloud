The rule lives in `registry_versions.values_problem`, and `registry/versions.py` is unchanged. Request: confirm.
- **L3-1-Q-3.** The start of a future open period (04 §16.5; POLICIES §0.5 rule 3). [J] For a version holding a pin P parameter:
  - For every active legal entity in scope (the entity for ENTITY; every active entity otherwise), the date of `effective_from` in the entity's time zone is the start date of a period of its calendar.
  - That period has not started, and its state for the book (a BOOK version's book, otherwise the primary book) is `future` or `open`.
  - Without entities, `effective_from` is later than now. The time of day is not checked.
  - The rule is checked on create, PATCH, `/test`, the job and `/submit`, not at approval.
  - Error: 422 `validation-failed`, field `effective_from`, rule T-PLT-32.
  - Request: confirm, including whether local midnight should be required.
- **L3-1-Q-4.** The preset.
  - [J] `POST /policies/presets/legacy-parity` names no category (04 §16.5), and a version holds one category, so the preset creates the `ACCOUNTING_POLICY` version at the requested scope.
  - Its values are every parameter of that category that a version of the scope may hold (level allowed; not forced at that scope) and that has a parity value.
  - POL-026 `material_right.ssp_method` (parity ENTERED_AMOUNT; levels P, C, O), named in the acceptance, cannot be held by a TENANT version. It is absent, and the test asserts its absence.
  - Parity values of other categories are not in the version: POL-021 `FALSE` (practical expedient) and `integration.grouping_fields`.
  - `book` defaults to ASC606 for BOOK scope and is refused (422 T-PLT-32) for TENANT and ENTITY.
  - `create_draft_version`, the shared DRAFT insert, lives in `registry/presets.py` beside `create_preset_version`.
  - Request: rule how the preset carries level P, C and O parity values and those of other categories (for example product or template `policy_values`, or one preset version per category).
- **L3-1-Q-5.** Testing a registry version.
  - [J] `POST /policies`, PATCH and `/test` validate synchronously, so the named 422 tests answer the request itself.
  - `/test` defers `POLICY_SIMULATION` with the content hash it saw. The runner refuses a changed version (409 REQ-POL-003), validates again and runs the simulation providers when `run_simulation` is true.
