### ENG-FIX 1: returns memo and price at the version date (52999f9)

Root cause (L4-3-Q-35). The T-CON-08 columns `transaction_price` and `expected_returns_amount` read `tp_history[-1]`, the price at the last reallocating boundary. Stage 09 reports `allocated_amount` = a_posted − round(r × (Y + E)) at d_v (S09-R-23). A `RETURN_RATE` version is measure-only (S08-R-02), and returns and window expiry are measure events, not boundaries. So the memo stayed at the earlier value, and the fail-closed S04-R-02 identity raised:
- `RET-CHK-060` 9,900.00 against 10,000.00 after the 31 January revision;
- `RET-JS-03` 100,500.00 against 100,000.00 after two returns;
- `SFC-S3-EX26-RETURN-RIGHT` 200.00 against 100.00 at the settlement invoice, after window expiry.

S04-R-08 and D-77 decision 2 measure the memo at a date on the Y + E of the stage 09 `ReturnState`, and EX-04-G2 states the figures (−300.00 / 9,700.00 after the returns; −200.00 / 9,800.00 at expiry).

Delivered:
- `s09_recognition/schedule.py`: `ObligationMeasures.returns_reduction` (defaulted) = the round(r × (Y + E)) that `_allocation` nets at d_v under `REDUCE_CONTRACT_QUANTITY` with a return path, else None. It comes from the new helper `_returns_reduction`.
- `erev_engine/__init__.py`: `_returns_memo` = −Σ `returns_reduction` over the obligations with a return path, None unless every one publishes it. `_contract_version` takes `measures` and, when the memo differs from `tp.expected_returns.posted`, sets `expected_returns_amount` to the memo and moves `transaction_price` by the difference.
