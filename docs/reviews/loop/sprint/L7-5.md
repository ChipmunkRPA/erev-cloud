### ENG-FIX 4: L5-3-Q-19 = L5-5-Q-17, terms-form modification lines (551bb39)

Root cause: dev-guide §9.5.4 terms-form lines (post-modification terms, `price_change_amount` the signed ΔC) went to stage 06 unchanged. `ModificationView.of` accepts only T-CON-06 delta lines ("obligation_key and action ADD, REMOVE or CHANGE are required").

Delivered (`runners.py`):
- `_terms_conversions` walks each contract's modifications in (effective date, key order) against the lines in force: the booked lines plus the earlier modifications' deltas.
- `_convert_terms` classifies each terms line:
  - ADD when the key is not in force: ΔQ = quantity, ΔC = total price.
  - REMOVE when the kind is REMOVE_OBLIGATION or TERMINATION, or line `end_date` < d: ΔQ = −(in force − net delivered), where `_net_delivered` is deliveries less returns on or before d.

- The in-force lines share D = `price_change_amount` − Σ ADD total price by largest remainder over |terms total − in-force total|, equal when all are 0 (`_largest_remainder`, ties in line order).
- The terms members carry over (dates, stratification, SSP label, memos; `performing_entity_code` as `selling_entity_code`, `account_overrides` as `account_codes`).
- `_modification_lines` feeds `ModificationInput.lines` and the `CONTRACT_AMENDED` payload. The converted `price_change_amount` is null, and `_implied_settlements` skips a converted modification. The run note lists the derived deltas.
