### Spec questions

- **L2-5-Q-1 (entry for late-event findings and register facts).** S08-R-10 to S08-R-13 publish `LATE_EVENT` findings, register facts and `close_gate_facts`, but Table 0.2-A names only `apply`, `assign_posting_period` and `decompose_prior_period`, and `AllocatedState` has no member for facts. Decision: `late_events(ctx, st, tb) -> LateEvents(findings, register, close_gate_facts)` is exported, run once per book over the folded state; the facts are stage 08 dataclasses (`LateEventFact`, `ReassessmentGap`). ENB-13 or the book loop binds the call and routes the facts to stage 14 and the platform.
- **L2-5-Q-2 (value of `late.assign.v1`).** §8.6 lists the formula, but an assignment is a pair of period keys. Decision: node `late_assign@<event key>:<contract>:-` per new late event with a posting period; the value is the number of periods carried, re-evaluated from `states` (the E-04 states from the origin through the posting period), with the keys in params.
- **L2-5-Q-3 (POL-042 key).** S08-R-13 reads `vc.reassessment_gate` (pin P), but Table 0.10-A row 08 does not declare it, and the stage key of S13-R-04 covers only declared keys. Decision: `POLICY_KEYS` of stage 08 adds it.
- **L2-5-Q-4 (posted amounts for S08-R-15).** `decompose_prior_period` needs `InputBundle.posted`, but neither its signature nor `AllocatedState` carries it. Decision: (a) keyword `posted: PostedIndex | None = None`, bound by stage 15 from `CanonicalBundle.posted`, as `price_at` is (L1-3-Q-26); a carry without it fails closed. (b) "Recomputed target through t − 1 less posted through t − 1" is applied to each `closed` or `permanently_locked` period p before t whose carry `assign_posting_period` posts in t: period target of p (stage 09 after S09-R-41) less the revenue posted with origin p (debit positive, so negated; S14-R-04). This equals the rule text when every earlier period carries into t, and never counts a difference that posts in its own open period.
- **L2-5-Q-5 (measure-only versions; EX-15-C).** S08-R-14 covers versions that change progress totals, such as `EAC`, but cost-to-cost progress (ENC-5) is deferred post-rc, so stage 09 reads no EAC pin, and a measure-only version adds no segment (S08-R-02). Decision: parts come from segments created in the period; measure-only versions give 0.00 until stage 09 reads their totals. EX-15-C is measured with `UNITS_DELIVERED` (costs as units, EAC as contracted quantity), and the EAC change is an `ESTIMATE_CHANGE` segment carrying the new totals, as the §8.1 outputs allow.
- **L2-5-Q-6 (termination and 25-13(a) parts).** Evaluating a `TERMINATION` segment at s would recognise its whole remaining allocation (S09-R-06), and a new `PROSPECTIVE` base at s is undefined. Decision: a 25-13(a) boundary in t, a price change copying such a base, and a termination each give 0.00 (CV-63; S15-R-13). A `PROSPECTIVE` price change on a base in force at s is measured from that base's boundary.
- **L2-5-Q-7 (quarter periods in CHK-110).** The PT-01 table is by quarter, but E-50 has no quarterly pattern. Decision: the test builds calendar-quarter periods under the `P445` literal, which only selects the non-`MONTHLY` branch of ALG-11 rule 3.
- **L2-5-Q-8 (open VC element and "effective at the period end").** No bundle member marks a VC element as resolved. Decision: an element is open from its first version's effective date through the horizon when its kind is `VARIABLE_CONSIDERATION` and its target is not `INCREMENTS`. A version satisfies a period end when its `effective_date` equals the period end date; a `no_change_attestation` version is such a version.
- **L2-5-Q-9 (subject and detail of `LATE_EVENT`).** S08-R-10 names the detail but no subject. Decision: the obligation subject key when the event names one obligation (the answer keys list the finding per obligation), else the contract subject key; detail `event_key`, `origin_period_key` and `posting_period_key` ("-" when absent), `reason` (`CLOSED_PERIOD`, `OUT_OF_ORDER`, or both), `rule`. "Recorded before it" compares `record_seq` within the contract.

### Spec questions

- **L2-5-Q-28 (pass name and the `CLOSE_RELEASE` trigger).** §14.1 names the pass of a close run as an input, but `BookContext` has no member for it, and S14-R-05 does not say whether a release run carries closed-period amounts. Decision: `run` takes the keyword `pass_name`. It is required under `CLOSE_RELEASE` and refused under a compute trigger. `CLOSE_RELEASE` posts `TIME` deltas of postable periods through the horizon (the close period) for the role keys whose parts have a `TIME` driver in that pass, and it carries no closed-period amounts (S14-R-05 wording). A role key shared by two passes, such as JET-10d at period end and at window expiry, posts its whole `TIME` delta in whichever pass runs first; because every path posts target minus posted, nothing posts twice (RCP-08). A posted-only role key takes its pass from its entry kind. `posted` is required: without it `run` raises `ENGINE_INVARIANT_VIOLATED` (rule S14-R-04), as L2-5-Q-4 decided for stage 08.
- **L2-5-Q-29 (splitting a cumulative target into posting classes).** S14-R-04 compares targets and posted amounts per class, but a part target can combine `EVENT` and `TIME` amounts, and no producer publishes the split. Decision: each `PartTarget` carries its cumulative `TIME` share, and `EVENT` is the rest. Relief: the `NORMAL` relief of obligations in `PartInputs.released_at_close` (T-ENG-02 `is_released_at_close`); modifications, estimate changes and other causes are `EVENT`. JET-13 performing: all `TIME` when the obligation is released at close, else all `EVENT`. JET-10a, 10a′, 10b and 10d: the period-end remeasurements (movement reason `PERIOD_END`), so settlement differences are `EVENT`. JET-06: `TIME`. JET-03, JET-04b and JET-05c: `EVENT`; window-expiry `TIME` needs a stage 10 expiry split. A foreign-currency functional `TIME` share is proportional to the transaction split, rounded half up, with the rest to `EVENT`. JET-10d expiry settlements are therefore `EVENT` until stage 12 publishes the expiry reason.
- **L2-5-Q-30 (void reason and posting kind).** S14-R-08 gives `reason_code = VOID` and posting kind `VOID_REVERSAL` "for `CONTRACT_VOIDED`", while the acceptance voids a delivery event. The consumed state keeps no void (stage 01 removes both events). Decision: `run` takes the keyword `voided`, the member contracts whose new events include `EVENT_VOIDED` or `CONTRACT_VOIDED`, bound by the book loop. Every delta of their subjects carries `VOID`, carries included, and `PostingState.posting_kind` is `VOID_REVERSAL` when any delta does. It is otherwise the pass name or `ENGINE_COMPUTE`.
- **L2-5-Q-31 (effective dates).** §14.1 lists an effective date on each intent and S14-R-07 dates the JET-06 lines, but `bundle.PostingIntent` (dev-guide §7 field list) has no member for it. Decision: `PostingState.effective_dates` maps entry key → date: the origin period's end for a carry, the posting period's end for a native delta, and the posting period's first day for `NETTING_RECLASS_REVERSAL`.
- **L2-5-Q-32 (agent split weights).** Table 14-A splits the agent credit by "the stage 03 gross-to-net basis", but stage 03 never fills `ObligationState.gross_to_net`, and only a commission rate gives weights. Decision: `gross_to_net` = {`gross_to_net_basis`: `COMMISSION_RATE`, `rate`} gives weights `rate` : 1 − `rate` over (`REVENUE`, `BILLING_CLEARING:AP_SUPPLIER`); any other basis refuses (`ValueError`).
- **L2-5-Q-33 (T-REF-15 steps 1 and 2; the node value).** Stage 03 merges the template's `account_role_overrides` below the line's overrides into `ObligationState.account_overrides`, so stage 14 cannot tell step 1 from step 2, and a node value must be a number. Decision: step 1 covers both overrides. The `account_resolution` node's value is the step (1 or 3); the account code and mapping version key travel in params, and the input is the override or rule reference.
- **L2-5-Q-34 (line side with mixed signs).** `IntentLine` carries one side for both currencies, but a delta's transaction and functional amounts can differ in sign after rounding at different rates. Decision: the side follows the transaction amount, else the functional one, and opposite signs raise `ENGINE_INVARIANT_VIOLATED` (rule S14-R-10).
- **L2-5-Q-35 (rate key on a line).** REQ-FX-006 stamps the rate on each line, but `bundle.IntentLine` has no rate member, and a cumulative delta can combine several rates (L2-5-Q-13).
