Spec questions:
- **L2-2-Q-14** Several adjusted obligations in one contract (S04-R-10, S04-R-12, S04-R-13).

  - T is the transfer date the adjusted obligations share. When their dates differ, T is the stated-price-weighted mean of their ordinal days, floored to a date; when every price is 0, T is the earliest date.

  - No named test has more than one adjusted obligation.
- **L2-2-Q-15** `SFC_SCHEDULE_UNSETTLED`.
  - A payment enters the balance at the grid step equal to its count: n months, or 12 × ⌊months ÷ 12⌋ under `ANNUAL`. So a schedule built from its own payment points always settles exactly, and S04-INV-04 is asserted.
  - The finding follows 05 §3.6.3 ("payment events exceed the financed balance"): it is raised by the first cash `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` that takes cumulative receipts past the settlement amount at its date by more than one minor unit.
  - For a deferred schedule, the settlement amount is the scheduled payments through the step of the month end on or after the receipt, plus the balance after that step. For an advance, it is Σ scheduled payments.
  - A receipt on or before its due date never raises the finding.
