### Spec questions

- **L3-2-Q-1 (refusal scope in `apply`).** ENB-13 asks `apply` to refuse integrity codes, but TC-pob-vc-08 (quantity +2 with billing −200 on a POB-specific price change) expects `VC_QUANTITY_NOT_ALLOWED` as a state finding. Refusing `MOD_SIGN_MISMATCH` would pre-empt it. CV-44 leaves type, cross-row and file codes to the import pipeline, and lets the engine re-check only what the whole stream can break.
  - Decision: `apply` refuses the cross-file codes `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` and `POB_NOT_FOUND`, and a modification already applied (`MOD_DUPLICATE_KEY`). `MOD_SIGN_MISMATCH`, duplicate lines and `IMPORT_NO_DATA_ROWS` stay validator codes.
  - "Already applied" means an earlier `CONTRACT_AMENDED` or `CONTRACT_TERMINATED` of the contract with the same `modification_id`, or an obligation of the contract whose `last_modification_key` is the key.
  - `REGROUPED`, `LINE_ATTRIBUTES_CHANGED` and `MATERIAL_RIGHT_EXERCISED` are checked for the contract only.
- **L3-2-Q-2 (stage 04 price binding).** Stage 04 `price_at` takes a `PobState` and reads booking lines only (L2-3-Q-1, L2-3-Q-32).
  - Decision: the binding adds Σ `consideration_delta` of the T-CON-06 lines of every `CONTRACT_AMENDED` and `CONTRACT_TERMINATED` effective on or before `at` and before the position, and `additional_consideration` of every such `MATERIAL_RIGHT_EXERCISED`, to `fixed`, `total` and `allocation_basis`.
  - r of a returnable obligation (L2-2-Q-6) is x_exact ÷ Q of its `FIXED` segment in force, with Q the obligation's quantity after the modifications applied so far.
  - Not built: the consideration of a `REGROUPED` after posting; and "versions effective at d that the modification makes includable" (L2-3-Q-23). The real stage 04 includes a version only after its `ESTIMATE_CHANGED`, so ΔVC is 0 at the amendment and the version enters through stage 08.
