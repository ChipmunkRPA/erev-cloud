### Spec questions

- **L2-4-Q-1 (S09-INV-07 check).** "An obligation version carrying two recognition methods" has no member of its own: `recognition_method` is one literal. Decision: the `FIXED` segments of an obligation name at most one E-11 method in `progress_measure`; `UNITS_SINCE_BOUNDARY` and `SSP_DELIVERED` are markers since a boundary (S06-R-29, S07-R-04) and do not count. The segment measure is not compared with `recognition_method`, because the stage 09 test builders default segments to `TIME_ELAPSED` for point-in-time obligations.
- **L2-4-Q-2 (version date).** §9.1 and Table 0.9-A evaluate the measures at d_v, but no `BookContext` or state member carries it. Decision: d_v is the latest effective date of the included events (04 T-CON-11 `effective_date`, "latest effective date included"), else the group inception date. Stage 09 and stage 10 measures use it. The catch-up nodes of L1 are evaluated at the horizon end and hold the same values, because every boundary is an included event dated on or before d_v.
- **L2-4-Q-3 (scheduled versus awaiting under guards and returns).** Decisions: only a time-elapsed `FIXED` component that recognises at d_v, or that awaits `recognition_start_date` (V5), is scheduled; a V4 status (including the S02-R-05 freeze), a pending custodial start and an open recognition hold leave the remainder awaiting a trigger. Realised `PERIOD_VC` amounts are part of A and of revenue. Under POL-053 `REDUCE_CONTRACT_QUANTITY` with a return path, `allocated_amount` = a_posted − round(r × (Y + E)) (S09-R-23).
- **L2-4-Q-4 (legacy and activity measures).** Decisions: the unit SSP is `AllocationSegment.unit_ssp` (0 when absent, as in the stage 09 test builders); `ssp_delivered_cum` sums each delivery and return at the unit SSP of the segment in force at its position, which reduces to S09-R-47 with one segment. `delivered_quantity` and `ssp_delivered` count the new deliveries less new returns (LM-CL-54, LM-CL-59). `revenue_amount` is the posted effect of the new events at their ENG-06 positions, catch-ups included (LM-CL-55), so a billing-only version gives 0 (CV-64; DEV-051).
- **L2-4-Q-5 (schedule line members).** §9.2.14 names `exact_by_type`, `units_in_period` and `is_deterministic_period_amount` without bodies. Decisions: `cumulative_exact` of a line type other than `NORMAL` is its running amount; the `NORMAL` line takes the exact target less those, so the line types sum to the exact target. `quantity` is the net units delivered in the period under a units measure, else absent. `is_released_at_close` is true for the `NORMAL` line of a time-elapsed obligation in a period whose realised `PERIOD_VC` amount did not change.
- **L2-4-Q-6 (satisfaction status).** Decisions: `CANCELLED` when a `TERMINATION` segment is in force at d_v or `terminated_on` ≤ d_v (S06-R-21 gives each ended obligation that segment). `satisfied_date` is the first date of the latest run of completion over the dates at which progress can change (events, boundaries, term ends, period ends, the day after a return window).
- **L2-4-Q-7 (BalanceState content).** §0.4 names only `allocated` as carried; stages 11 and 15 read stage 09 targets. Decision: `BalanceState.recognition` carries the `RecognitionState`. `paid_cum` has no formula in §10.5, so it is a mapping on (contract@entity, period key) without trace nodes, and the receipts are exposed. JET-03 reads "unconditional billing and tax (10)", but §10.5 publishes no tax measure: the document lines carry `tax_amount` and unconditional dates, and no `sales_tax_payable` target is built.
- **L2-4-Q-8 (billing document identity).** A payload holds one line. Decisions: the lines of one contract with the same kind, number and effective date form one document and are apportioned once; a credit memo naming `credited_invoice_number` is weighted by the attribution of the earlier invoice documents with that number; a status update is recognised in ENG-06 order; an absent `is_cancellable` means noncancellable.
- **L2-4-Q-9 (remaining billing since a boundary).** S10-R-05 reads "plan of the segment in force − `billed_cum`", but S06-R-29 and S07-R-04 already store the remaining plan at the boundary. Decision: on the inception basis `remaining_billing` = plan − `billed_cum`; since a `PROSPECTIVE` boundary it is plan − the billing attributed after the boundary event.
- **L2-4-Q-10 (NOT_A_CONTRACT billing in `ERP` mode).** S10-R-08 says B_u = Σ `billed_cum` in `ERP` mode, while S10-R-04 keeps billing recorded while `NOT_A_CONTRACT` out of any position. Decision: the S10-R-04 exclusion applies in both modes, so the `ERP` identity holds outside those intervals.
- **L2-4-Q-11 (billing mode resolution).** POL-004 and POL-123 are pin `P`, and the `DEFAULT` preset carries no POL-123 value (no framework default). Decision: the mode of a line is read at the contracting entity's period of its effective date, from POL-123 when present, else derived from POL-004. The unconditional date is the later of the line date and the earliest applied payment or status update.
- **L2-4-Q-12 (attribution weights).** Decision: the posted allocation at the document date is `a_posted` of the `FIXED` segment in force by effective date, read in stage 10 because DG-ENG-07 forbids stage 09 private helpers; when those sum to 0, the resolved SSPs; with no candidate obligation, `ENGINE_INVARIANT_VIOLATED` (rule S10-R-01).

### Spec questions

- **L2-4-Q-37 (acceleration date and ρ).** S11-R-07 starts clawback and acceleration segments on "their effective date", while S11-R-13 accelerates carrying(d_T). CHK-112 (18,000.00 at month 18) terminates on the EX-06-J date 30 June 2027, whose `TERMINATION` segment keeps the revenue of that day. Decisions:
  - A clawback or an acceleration dated d is measured on the carrying amount after amortisation through d; its segment starts on d + 1 day.
  - ρ = Σ after ÷ Σ before over the related obligations, capped at 1:
    - An obligation with a `TERMINATION` segment of the event: before = the posted allocation before less that segment's posted allocation (C_before(d_T), no pool share); after = 0.
    - An obligation with another segment of the event: before and after are the posted allocations before and after, less the segment's base revenue.
    - Any other obligation: its posted allocation less its revenue target at the latest period end on or before d_T, in both sums.
  - ρ = 0 when every related obligation ended; 1 when the event names none of them, or when before is 0.
  - The new segment ends at the latest end date of the remaining related obligations: on its start date when none remains, and at the asset end when none has an end date.
- **L2-4-Q-38 (clawback matching and the unimpaired amount).** Decisions:
  - A clawback matches the `OBTAIN` assets of its contract with the same `payee` and `plan_code` that were capitalised on or before its date, oldest first (capitalisation date, asset key). The amount spills to the next asset; any excess beyond them stays in the ERP, without a finding.
  - A clawback without `payee` or `plan_code`, or a `cost_adjustment` other than `CLAWBACK` or on another purpose, fails closed (CV-45).
  - The IFRS reversal cap (S11-R-10 "first-segment schedule, net of clawbacks") is also net of acceleration.
- **L2-4-Q-39 (loss scope at a period end).** Decisions:

  - A unit tested earlier and out of scope at t is published with required 0.00 (param `in_scope` false), so JET-12 releases it.
- **L2-4-Q-40 (unit costs, EAC and consideration).** Decisions:
  - A `CONTRACT` unit counts every `PROGRESS_INPUT` cost and `EAC` element of the contract. A `POB` unit counts those naming the obligation, plus unnamed ones when it is the contract's only obligation.
  - The latest versions of several elements are summed. Under POL-152 `IAS37_68A_COSTS` the `EAC_IAS37` elements replace the others when present; under `SUBTOPIC_605_35_COSTS` they are ignored.

  - `measurement_basis` follows POL-152. Without an `EAC` version, `expected_total_costs` is 0.00 and `expected_margin` is TP_u.
