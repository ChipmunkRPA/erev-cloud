### 4.2 PRF-1 — interpretations recorded (no spec text changed; confirm or rule)

- **A. Events are not stored in the manifest JSON.** PERF-10 says the generator "produces a manifest JSON and the event set"; the manifest holds the parameters of every event and `events()` derives the ≈1.26 million events deterministically (streamed, contract by contract), so the JSON stays at 19 MB. Correction (Codex 2306 MANIFEST-BINDING-1): at 1be86b73 the hash did not fully identify the event set (the effective-day rule and the seeded FX rates were outside it); since 4.5 the manifest binds `counts.events_sha256` (SHA-256 over every event's canonical facts in stream order) and `recipe` (generator version, calendar, currencies, fiscal years, FX rates per month, seeded texts), so the dataset identity covers the generated reference values and the event facts. The idempotency key `perf:<hash16>:<seq>` numbers events in stream order.
- **B. "Month 24 carries 8% of all events" is a design outcome, not a constant.** With bookings spread evenly by inception month and schedules that run at least to the end of the 24-month horizon, the active base grows linearly and the last month carries about 2/25 of the events; a fiscal year-end usage true-up (5% of the year's rated amount, months 12 and 24) completes the load. Measured: 8.0% (test tolerance ±0.5 pp).
- **C. Two event types are deferred by `seed()` — the deferred count is NOT an exercised full mix (Codex 2306 §3).** `CONTRACT_AMENDED` needs an approved `modification_id` and `ESTIMATE_CHANGED` an approved `estimate_version_id` (04 §16.3 payload members); the batch append cannot create them. The manifest schedules them (2 prospective + 1 catch-up modification per 100 contract-years; VC changes on 3% of contracts per quarter; C2C EAC changes each quarter) and `seed()` counts them as `events_deferred`; PRF-2's seeding path routes them through the modification and estimate commands. Question 1.
- **D. Terms.** PERF-13 "TIME_ELAPSED daily over 12 to 36 months": drawn within 12 to 36 with 90% in 24 to 36 (B); usage, delivery, milestone, cost and instalment schedules run at least to the horizon end (capped at 36 months).
