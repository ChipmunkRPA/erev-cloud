## Deviations and notes

- Rule (b) as written in D-90b, S06-R-08 and table 15.4-C refuses any two same-date modifications moving one eligible started obligation's quantity in opposite directions, whatever their order. The "split removal-first equals the explicit representation" and W2 worlds of the D-90b test list therefore cannot run at one date; the lane records the removal the day before the addition, which reconstructs the explicit layers exactly (the layer starts are the only inputs to ρ), and asserts that both same-date orders are refused. See L9-ENG-Q-1.
- The finding detail members (`reason` with the literals `MIXED_SIGNS`, `OPPOSITE_SAME_DATE`, `RE_TERMED_ADDED_UNITS`; `added`, `removed`, `term_start`, `start_date`) are the lane's choice; no document defines them.
- Condition (c) accepts an added-unit line whose `start_date` equals either the booked term start (`seg.totals.start_date or ob.start_date`) or its `recognition_start_date`-clamped value, because neither re-terms the layers.
- The single-layer identity test is a plain engine test over every date of the term (3 × 3,653 evaluations), not a Hypothesis property; `backend/tests/properties` was not extended (its P-list is fixed by DG-PROP).
- `make setup` was not rerun (environment present); `make openapi` was not needed (no exposed enum lists finding codes).
- A worktree-root `.ruff_cache` created by direct `ruff` runs was removed before `make ci`; `make` targets use `backend/.ruff_cache` and `backend/.mypy_cache`.
