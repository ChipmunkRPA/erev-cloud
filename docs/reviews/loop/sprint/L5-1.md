[J] `CONTACT_FIELDS` names 16 members: the PRV-05 examples (`customer_email`, `customer_name`, `customer_phone`, `customer_address`, `contact_email`) and the plain and contact forms of the four kinds (`email`, `phone`, `address`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `full_name`, `contact_name`, `contact_phone`, `contact_address`, `billing_address`, `shipping_address`).
