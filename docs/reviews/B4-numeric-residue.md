### Decisions taken in the sweep (D-77)

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| B4-NR-01 | Month-end count, not the floor (item 1) | Discounting on the accretion grid is the only convention under which the schedule settles exactly and the oracle-verified figures of CHK-136 and CHK-137 hold |
| B4-NR-02 | Signed memo columns (≤ 0), not magnitudes (item 3) | Every identity in 04, the dev-guide and 05 subtracts `consideration_payable_amount`. Magnitudes would have required changing documents outside the corpus and this author's remit |
| B4-NR-03 | Obligation `revenue_cum` gross of consideration payable; contract `revenue_cum` net of the JET-14 release | The DB-17 obligation identity needs the gross allocation. GL revenue and the keys' contract figures are net |
| B4-NR-04 | S04-R-12a re-spreads the suspended interest by the inception schedule's weights | No catch-up, and it reproduces research 04 S3-EX26 (8.51 and 12.49) with integer powers only. A constant-yield rate over 21 months needs a fractional power (CV-31) |
| B4-NR-05 | `questionnaire` is required when the topic's T-CON-19 schema has required members, and its member set is exact | A reviewed record without those members cannot be submitted (04 T-CON-19); an exact set catches typos |
| B4-NR-06 | Questionnaire values where the key gives no facts: `consideration_nonrefundable` false; GE-06 `enforceable_end_date` 2027-01-31 and `termination_penalty_substantive` false; agent outcomes `FIXED_FEE` with the line amount | Under these values no figure moves: S02-R-07 events cannot fire, S02-R-06 truncates nothing (no `termination` member), and S03-R-09 leaves the stated price unchanged |
