## 6. Method

- The word-level diff of every changed document and key (`.scratch/b4-buildspec-impact/compact.txt`) gave the changed ids, values and literals. Each was grepped in `docs/BUILD_SPEC.md` with `hits.py`, which maps every hit to its item id and template field (`hits/*.txt`, 55 pattern files). `.scratch/b4-buildspec-impact/verify_fragments.py` confirms that all 91 BUILD_SPEC and 17 PHASES old-text fragments occur verbatim on their cited lines (0 failures), and `fix_review.py` escapes pipes inside code spans and checks every table row's cell count (0 problems).
- Verbatim old text was taken from the merged file at HEAD. New figures were taken from the owner texts at rev 1.3 (ENGINE_SPEC EX-04-G2, EX-04-H, S04-R-12, S04-R-12a; ENGINE_SPEC_B EX-11-A; PRD WLD-X-24 and J-02; SCREENS §4.4 and §11.4) and from the key files (`SFC-S3-EX26-RETURN-RIGHT`, `RET-CHK-029-S3-EX22`, `CPC-CHK-133-S3-EX32`, `POB-JS-05-*`).
- Not run: `merge_buildspec.py`, `check_buildspec.py` and `check_merged.py`, which the brief does not list among the permitted scripts. Their expected results (note 3 above; §3) are inferred from the checker source.
