- **Q.** One entity contracts and another entity of the group performs one of the obligations. The performing entity keeps a book — IFRS 15, say — that the contracting entity does not keep. What does that book record for the obligation: nothing, the performing entity's revenue under a rule still to be written, or is such a contract refused?
- **Readings.** None is ruled. R-99 (b) registers the number and the question — "what the book of a performing entity records when the contracting entity does not keep that book" — and adds "note owed by lane QA-BE". The ruling cites no authority. No note of that lane on the item is among its reports to the supervisor, and no tracked file but the rulings log and the supervisor's register names the item.
- **Today.** [L, lane SECFIX-ACT, message to the supervisor of 2026-09-30 20:08 UTC] Measured in a world in which "AVM-UK performs a line and keeps IFRS15, AVM-US contracts and does not": "the booking itself is QUARANTINED — ENGINE_INVARIANT_VIOLATION", the reason given being "CV-13 (the period carries no state for the book: book_code IFRS15, period_key FY2026-P09)". [F] CV-13 is the engine's horizon rule: the horizon of an entity and book is the last period whose state is open, closing or reopened (`docs/accounting/ENGINE_SPEC.md:208`), and the engine expects a state of every period for every book the entity keeps: a period that carries none for the book raises the invariant by name (`backend/erev_engine/dates.py:180–192`). So, by the lane's measurement, such a contract is quarantined when it is booked and nothing is computed for it.
- **Evidence.** None measured by this lane; the case needs a database world. No figure.
- **Status.** Not ruled. Registered by R-99 (b) as a candidate; nothing is built for it and the lane's note is owed.
- **If the accountant decides.** That such a contract must be computed: a rule for what each book of each entity records for it, in the engine and in the bundle assembly that gives the engine the period states of a book one of the entities does not keep. That it need not be: a refusal by name when the contract is booked, in place of a quarantined computation.

### 3.3 AD-56 (candidate) — Step 1 after activation: which book the criteria-met path serves, and an assessment that waits for its judgement's review (items CTR-STEP1-REENTRY-1, STEP1-JR-STALE-1)

- **Q.** (1) A contract is ACTIVE and one of its books is not a contract — the books differed at activation, or collectibility was reassessed later. When the criteria are met in that book, the product is to move it by an approved request. R-102 (a): "in the first release the path serves a book the engine put out as NOT_PROBABLE only; a book out for another reason (no commercial substance) is refused by name and stays a known limitation". Is that limit acceptable? (2) R-102 (b) lets a Step 1 assessment cite only a judgement record that has been reviewed. The not-a-contract accounting, and a reassessment under 606-10-25-5, then wait for a second person. Is that wait acceptable, and what should the periods between the first indication and the recorded assessment show? The ruling's sentence for the accountant ends: "an open period is corrected by the recompute, a period locked in between is not."
- **Readings.** (1) As ruled: the same activation request admits an ACTIVE header; on approval a criteria-met event is appended per moving book, "dated the re-assessment's date"; "the recompute posts the catch-up (POL-013)". (2) As ruled, "an assessment cites a REVIEWED judgement record, on every channel". The alternative, a record that is only submitted, is not taken because it "would let a review be decided on a stream the reviewer did not see". Authority the ruling cites: 606-10-25-5 for the reassessment; POL-013 (`docs/accounting/POLICIES.md:206`) for the catch-up, which cites 606-10-25-6 and 25-8.
- **Today.** [F] On main an ACTIVE contract cannot be submitted for activation: the admitted states are draft, pending review and not a contract (`backend/erev_api/domain/contracts/activation.py:205–209`; the refusal at `:804–805`). The contract's header moves on a criteria-met event only from not a contract (`backend/erev_api/events/stream.py:204–205`). At activation the engine puts a book out for one of three reasons (`backend/erev_engine/stages/s02_contract_identification/status.py:144–154`): not probable, no commercial substance, or both parties free to terminate with nothing performed. A book out for the third reason returns by itself with the first performance (`:121–126`). So a book that is out as not probable, or for want of commercial substance, under an ACTIVE header has no way back today. An assessment may cite a record that is "`SUBMITTED` or `REVIEWED`" (`docs/04-DATA_MODEL.md:6183`). Both rules of R-102 are built and not merged: branch `sprint/w11`, commit `deb8e8b6`, its documents in `e4c45f14`.
- **Evidence.** No figure; none is in the log and the lane measured none. One sentence of R-102 (b) did not describe the code and is corrected by R-118 (b) [F]. R-102 (b) said for the accountant: "an ACTIVE book goes on recognising until the reviewed not-probable assessment is recorded". R-118 (b): "The code on main is the fact" — "recognition therefore continues only between the flag and the submission; the review releases the hold and catches up; the not-probable assessment recorded next takes the book out, from a date that may lie back at the flag". On main the submission of a judgement record that names a contract places a system recognition hold on it, unless the record is approved automatically (`backend/erev_api/domain/policies/judgements.py:402–408`), for an ACTIVE contract only (`backend/erev_api/domain/contracts/holds.py:352–358`); the review releases the hold (`backend/erev_api/domain/policies/judgements.py:551–556`). The witnesses on main are `backend/tests/domain/contracts/test_holds.py:105` and `:217`. [L, lane SECFIX-ACT, residuals report 7/7 of 2026-10-01 02:26 UTC] The lane, which had first written the sentence, adds: "What the accountant should be shown: a catch-up at the review that the following assessment reverses, both in the open period unless a lock falls between the two steps", and "I have not measured the figures".
- **Status.** R-102 (a): "an accounting point pending the accountant (candidate AD-56)"; R-102 (b): "For the accountant (candidate AD-56, with (a))". Built, not merged. **Supervisor ruling pending the independent accountant.**

- **Ruled, not built.** `git log --all` finds no commit that builds the item — a commit that only mentions it is not counted — and the place on main where the rule would act still holds the earlier behaviour, which the packet anchors. Work that a lane holds uncommitted in its own tree cannot be seen and is not counted.
