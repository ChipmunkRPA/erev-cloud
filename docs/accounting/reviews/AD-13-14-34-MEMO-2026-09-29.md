### 3.4 Analysis (AD-34)

Arithmetic.

- Fixed revenue for the quarter: 2,000,000.00 × 3 ÷ 12 = 500,000.00. Royalty: 1,000,000.00 × 5% = 50,000.00. Revenue 550,000.00 under every treatment.
- T1: 2,000,000.00 + 50,000.00 = 2,050,000.00; 2,050,000.00 − 550,000.00 = 1,500,000.00, which is the nine months of fixed fee not yet recognised. The royalty enters and leaves the allocation in the quarter in which it is realised, because the licence is already partly satisfied. (A statement that arrives before any satisfaction stays in the allocation as an awaiting-trigger amount until the licence starts: measured on a scratch variant whose term begins on 1 April, which gives `transaction_price` 2,050,000.00, `remaining_allocation` 2,050,000.00 and awaiting trigger 50,000.00 at 31 March.)
- T2: 2,000,000.00 − 550,000.00 = 1,450,000.00. The subtraction takes royalty revenue of 50,000.00 out of a price that never contained it. The result understates the unearned fixed fee by exactly the royalty.
- The same rule at 31 December, with no further royalty: T1 gives 2,050,000.00 − 2,050,000.00 = 0.00; T2 gives 2,000,000.00 − 2,050,000.00 = −50,000.00, a negative remaining allocation on a fully satisfied obligation.

Findings.

1. **T2 is internally inconsistent [F, derived].** The key asserts a `NORMAL` schedule of 166,666.67 / 166,666.66 / 166,666.67 for January to March, that is a fixed fee of 2,000,000.00 recognised evenly, of which 1,500,000.00 remains. It also asserts `remaining_allocation` 1,450,000.00. Both cannot be true of one obligation: REQ-REC-021 and DB-17 require allocated = recognised + scheduled + awaiting trigger, and S09-INV-03 requires each term to be non-negative. The key asserts neither the scheduled nor the awaiting amount, so the inconsistency is derived from its schedule lines; it is not one assertion contradicting another. The engine refuses the combination outright (`backend/erev_engine/stages/s09_recognition/schedule.py:464–474`).
2. **T2 contradicts the published rules on the other two fields [F].** S04-R-02, S04-R-06 and S09-R-02 place realised royalties in the transaction price and in the obligation's allocation. The engine implements them (`backend/erev_engine/stages/s04_transaction_price/buildup.py:110–211`, `:330–335`, `:342–350`; `s09_recognition/schedule.py:449–463`).
