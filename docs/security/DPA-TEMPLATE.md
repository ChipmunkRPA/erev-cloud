# eRev Cloud data processing agreement template

**DRAFT for Legal and privacy-owner review. Not legal advice.** Feature descriptions state what the software does; the clauses are a starting point for the parties' counsel and bind nobody until executed.

A template of the GDPR Article 28(3) clauses for a hosted deployment of eRev Cloud, mapped to the product features that implement each obligation (05 §6.17 PRV-02, PRV-07, PRV-09 to PRV-12; BUILD_SPEC SOP-9). The tenant is the controller and the hosting operator the processor (PRV-02). The bracketed fields are external inputs (readiness register UI-14) and are left blank by the build loop. This template is a starting point for the parties' counsel; it is not legal advice, and the feature descriptions state what the software does, not what the parties agree.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Controller | `<UI-14: tenant legal entity, address, contact>` |
| Processor | `<UI-14: hosting operator legal entity, address, contact>` |
| Subject matter | Hosting and operation of the eRev Cloud revenue-recognition application for the controller's workspace(s) |
| Duration | The term of the service agreement, plus the deletion or return period of clause (g) |
| Nature and purpose | Storage and processing of the controller's contract, billing, revenue and accounting data and of its users' identities, to compute and report revenue under ASC 606 and IFRS 15 and to operate the controller's close |
| Categories of data | Business and financial data (C2); personal data of the controller's users and, where the controller enters it, of its customers' contacts: identity and login data, session and security-log metadata, names in documents (C3), per `erev_api.privacy.CLASSIFICATION` |
| Categories of data subjects | The controller's employees and contractors with workspace access; natural persons named in the controller's contracts, documents and imports |
| Region | `<UI-14: deployment region>` (PRV-10: one region per deployment) |

## Article 28(3) clauses mapped to features

| Clause | Obligation | Feature that implements it | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Documented instructions | The processor processes personal data only on the controller's documented instructions, including transfers | The controller instructs through its own configuration: workspace settings, roles and role assignments, integration connections, the AI switch (D-46, off by default), retention settings and the erasure commands. The processor runs no command in a workspace without a support grant approved by the controller (REQ-PLT-036) | `audit_event` rows of every configuration change with actor, roles and MFA flag (REQ-PLT-018); `support_grant` rows and their approvals |
| (b) Confidentiality | Persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality | Operator staff hold no standing access to tenant data (TB-7). Access exists only under a time-limited, read-only support grant approved by a Tenant Admin (72 hours maximum), and every action under it is written to the controller's audit log; `erev_owner` is held by the deployment pipeline (RB-11). `<UI-14: staff confidentiality undertaking reference>` | Support session log (CU-09); `security_event` `PLATFORM_SCOPE_USED` |
| (c) Security (Art. 32) | Appropriate technical and organisational measures | TLS 1.2+ at the edge (SAR-01); forced row-level security per tenant and per transaction (TXN-01, CTL-036); argon2id passwords and TOTP MFA with lockout (SAR-06, REQ-PLT-004); per-file encryption with wrapped data keys (PRV-06, KEY-04); HMAC-chained audit and security logs with daily verification and digests (DB-09, SAR-31); secrets by reference only (ARC-11); the SSRF guard on outbound calls (SAR-15); the upload defences (UPL-01 to UPL-12); the threat model and ASVS checklist (`docs/security/threat-model.md`, `docs/security/ASVS-L2.md`); an independent penetration test before the first production tenant (SAR-43, UI-11) | `erev doctor` output (REQ-CTL-005); the gate reports named in the release manifest (REQ-CTL-003); the security documents |
| (d) Sub-processors | No sub-processor without the controller's prior authorisation; notice of changes; flow-down of obligations | `docs/security/SUBPROCESSORS.md` lists Google Cloud, Anthropic (only for a controller that enables AI) and the configured SMTP relay, and nothing else; a change to the list is notified before it takes effect. The controller's own systems reached by adapters are not sub-processors | The sub-processor list and its change history in the repository |
| (e) Assistance with data-subject requests | Assist the controller in responding to requests under Chapter III (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) | See "Assistance with data-subject requests" below | Runbook RB-14; `app_user.anonymise` and `file_object.shred` audit events |
| (f) Assistance with Art. 32 to 36 | Security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments, prior consultation | Breach notification to affected controllers without undue delay, target 24 hours from confirmation, so the controller can meet its 72-hour duty (PRV-12, RB-10); the threat model, ASVS checklist and classification catalogue as inputs to the controller's impact assessment; `erev doctor` and the release manifest as evidence of the measures in force | Incident record (RB-10); the security documents; the release manifest |
| (g) Deletion or return at the end of the services | Delete or return all personal data and delete existing copies, unless retention is required by law | Return: the tenant export (REQ-RPT-028) delivers the controller's data in documented formats. Deletion: the operator drops the workspace's database (or the tenant's rows in a pooled database), deletes the tenant-prefixed objects of the file store including `.dek` sidecars, and lets backup and soft-delete retention lapse (OPR-10); accounting records the controller must retain leave with the export first (PRV-09) | Export manifest with SHA-256 sums (REQ-RPT-028); the operator's deletion record `<UI-14: deletion attestation form>` |
| (h) Audit and information | Make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance; allow and contribute to audits | Evidence packs (REQ-RPT-025) and the audit-chain verification digests (SAR-31) are available to the controller from the product; the operator provides its deployment self-check output and release manifests on request; a SOC report for the hosted service is later (03 §6.4 Assurance, REQ-CTL-009) | Evidence packs; `audit_chain_verification` rows; release manifests |

## Assistance with data-subject requests (Art. 28(3)(e); 05 PRV-07)

The controller decides every request; the processor assists. eRev Cloud 1.0 has no self-service erasure endpoint; erasure is the controller's own administrators running two audited commands (runbook RB-14), and the processor's assistance is limited to what the controller instructs under a support grant.

| Request type | What the product provides | Limits recorded, not decided (D-97 §C proposals pending the controller's privacy owner) |
|---|---|---|
| Access and portability | `GET /api/v1/users/{membership_id}` and the user listing show the identity and roles held; the audit log shows the person's actions with pseudonymous references; the tenant export (REQ-RPT-028) delivers the data in documented formats | Audit and security events carry pseudonymous `actor_id` references (PRV-03) and the PRV-04 HMAC form of past e-mail values, which are not personal data in clear |
| Rectification | `PATCH /api/v1/users/{membership_id}` for an invitation's display name; identity data comes from the controller's identity provider under OIDC | Accounting records and audit events are immutable by design; a correction is a new event, never an edit |
| Erasure of an identity | `POST /api/v1/users/{membership_id}/anonymise` (05 PRV-07 a): the person's display name and e-mail are replaced by the erased forms, password and external id cleared, status `DISABLED`, every session ended and every membership removed across the operator's workspaces; audited in each | MFA factors and recovery codes are not deleted in 1.0 (proposal: delete them); `access_review_item.user_email_snapshot`, `file_object.original_filename` and `contract_cost_asset.payee` are retained (proposals: HMAC form, id-based name, retention under Art. 17(3)(b)/(e) with the denial logged) |
| Erasure of documents | `POST /api/v1/files/{id}/shred` (05 PRV-07 b): the wrapped data key is destroyed and the bytes become unreadable at once; the row and its SHA-256 stay as evidence; irreversible after backup and soft-delete retention lapse (7 days hosted, OPR-10) | Refused while a legal hold or a retention date applies (`FILE_RETENTION_ACTIVE`); a legal hold prevails; plaintext-stored purposes (report outputs, evidence packs, journal exports, digests) cannot be shredded in 1.0 (rule `PRV-06`, unruled) |
| Restriction and objection | Suspension of a membership (`POST /api/v1/users/{membership_id}/suspend`) stops the person's access without erasing; AI processing of a workspace is switched off by the controller (D-46) | Accounting records, audit events and security events are retained for `platform.audit_retention_years` under the controller's legal obligations (PRV-09, Art. 17(3)(b) and (e)) |
| Timelines | The commands complete within the request; irreversibility of shredded documents follows in 7 days (OPR-10). The controller's one-month response period (Art. 12(3)) is the controller's to manage | `<UI-14: escalation contact at the processor for requests needing a support grant>` |

## Related provisions

- Data residency: one deployment, one region (PRV-10); the AI provider is a separate, disclosed transfer for controllers that enable AI.
- Retention: accounting records, audit events and security events for `platform.audit_retention_years` (default 7); security-log IP addresses and user agents with the security log under legitimate interest (PRV-09).
- Breach notification: PRV-12 (24-hour target from confirmation) and the incident runbook RB-10.
- Sub-processor list: `docs/security/SUBPROCESSORS.md`.
